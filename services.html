Architectural Consultation & Practice

Common questions regarding our architectural scope, consultation process, and project initiation.

What architectural services do you provide?

We provide comprehensive architectural planning, structural engineering, interior spatial design, and project coordination across six core typologies: Healthcare, Residential, Commercial, Hospitality, Industrial, and Institutional architecture. Each engagement encompasses conceptual planning, statutory compliance drawings, working engineering documentation, and construction site administration.

How can I discuss a new project with your team?

You can initiate a consultation by submitting an enquiry through our Contact Form or calling our Chennai studio directly at 044 2481 8999. Our senior architects review site parameters and arrange an initial preliminary briefing.

What information should I provide for an initial enquiry?

Helpful preliminary details include the site location, approximate plot dimensions or built-up area requirements, intended functional typology (e.g., healthcare facility, residence, or commercial complex), and any known zoning constraints. Site photographs or boundary surveys are also welcome if available.

How are project scope, professional fees, and milestones confirmed?

Following an initial consultation and site review, we prepare a detailed formal architectural proposal detailing the phased scope of work—from concept design and municipal sanction drawings through structural detailing and site monitoring. Professional fees and delivery milestones are determined transparently based on project scale and complexity.
