PRACTICE PRIVACY NOTICE

Website Privacy Policy

Entity: Aspirations Architects Engineers (Brand: Artech Design)
Practice Established: 1990 • Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India
Effective Date: October 2026 • Compliant with Indian Information Technology & Data Protection Principles
01.

Introduction

This Privacy Policy outlines how Aspirations Architects Engineers, practicing under the public-facing brand name Artech Design (“we”, “us”, or “our”), handles personal information gathered through our official website (arkitect.in) and related business communications.

Established in 1990 in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, our architectural and engineering consultancy is committed to integrity, transparency, and ethical client relationships. We respect your privacy and design our digital infrastructure to collect only the minimal information required to respond thoughtfully to your architectural planning and design enquiries.

02.

Information We Collect

We collect only personal information that visitors intentionally provide when requesting consultations, submitting project requirements, or communicating directly with our team. We do not engage in automated data scraping, covert tracking, or purchasing third-party marketing lists.

Data Category Specific Fields Collected Collection Source & Purpose
Contact Identification Full Name, Email Address, Contact Phone Number Voluntarily provided via our contact form to identify you and respond to your architectural enquiry.
Project & Organisation Company / Organisation name (optional), Architectural Vertical of interest, Project Location Helps our principal architects assess planning context, site parameters, and relevant typological expertise.
Project Requirements Detailed message or project brief text Outlines your facility scale, site constraints, and architectural aspirations to facilitate preliminary consultation.
Technical Transmission Data Client IP address, request timestamp, HTTP headers Automatically processed by our server infrastructure solely for rate limiting, DDoS prevention, and spam filtering.

Note on Verification: We do not collect credit card details, financial payment data, biometrics, or government identification numbers through this website.

03.

How We Use Information

Personal data provided to Aspirations Architects Engineers is utilized exclusively for legitimate professional architectural practice activities:

  • Responding to Enquiries: Reviewing your project requirements and contacting you via phone or email to discuss architectural planning, feasibility, or design proposals.
  • Client Communications: Coordinating preliminary site visits, architectural design reviews, and engineering scope discussions.
  • Service Delivery: Maintaining necessary records during ongoing design consultations and master planning workflows.
  • Security & Abuse Prevention: Implementing IP-based submission rate limits (maximum 5 submissions per 15-minute window) to protect our website from spam and automated flooding.
  • Legal & Regulatory Compliance: Satisfying statutory architectural record-keeping and professional standards established under Indian law.

We do not use your information for automated profiling, behavioral advertising, programmatic marketing campaigns, or algorithmic decision-making.

04.

Contact Forms and Enquiry Processing

When you submit an architectural enquiry via our Contact page, the submission workflow operates as follows:

  • Transmission: Your input is transmitted to our secure server endpoint (backend/api/contact.php) over encrypted HTTPS.
  • Sanitization & Validation: Data is validated and sanitized on the server against malicious injections and script attacks.
  • Spam Trapping: An invisible honeypot field captures and silently drops automated bot submissions without storing their data.
  • Logging & Routing: Verified enquiries are recorded into a secure, server-side audit log accessible exclusively to our senior practice administrators. Simultaneously, an internal notification is routed to our official mailbox (aspirations@arkitect.in).
  • External Services Status: Google Sheets automation and external CRM relays are currently not active on this website. Enquiries remain within our protected hosting and email infrastructure.
05.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website is designed with a privacy-first ethos. We do not use third-party tracking cookies, remarketing pixels, or behavioral analytics cookies (such as Google Analytics or Facebook Pixel).

The website relies solely on standard, non-tracking technical mechanisms:

  • Essential Technical Operation: Standard HTTP server transmission parameters that enable web pages to load efficiently.
  • Google Fonts: Web typography is delivered via Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). When your browser requests font files, Google receives standard HTTP connection details (IP address and user agent) subject to Google's standard font privacy terms.

Because this site does not deploy tracking, advertising, or analytical cookies, no intrusive cookie banner is required or displayed.

06.

Third-Party Services

We work only with essential technical infrastructure partners required to host and deliver our web services:

  • Web Hosting & Server Infrastructure: Our web server hosts the application, processes API requests, and enforces access control rules via .htaccess.
  • Email Transmission: Standard mail transport protocols (SMTP) used to route project enquiry alerts to our studio.
  • Typography Delivery: Google Fonts CDN for font delivery.

We do not embed third-party analytics trackers, social media sharing widgets that track visitors, or unverified third-party map trackers.

07.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

Aspirations Architects Engineers does not sell, rent, trade, or commercialize your personal information. We disclose personal data only under the following limited and lawful circumstances:

  • Internal Practice Personnel: Principal architects, project coordinators, and technical engineers who require the information to review and respond to your enquiry.
  • Technical Service Providers: Hosting and email delivery providers bound by professional duties of confidentiality, acting solely on our instructions.
  • Legal & Statutory Requirements: When disclosure is mandatory to comply with Indian statutory laws, court orders, judicial summons, or applicable regulatory mandates under the Council of Architecture.
08.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the specific purposes for which it was collected:

  • Preliminary Enquiries: Contact details and project inquiries that do not proceed to an active design agreement are retained only for a reasonable communication period to allow follow-up discussions, after which they are archived or deleted.
  • Active Client Projects: When an architectural mandate is established, project records, contractual correspondence, and design documentation are maintained in accordance with statutory limitation periods and architectural professional liability requirements in India.
09.

Data Security

We implement reasonable technical, administrative, and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information we process:

  • Encrypted Transport: All browser-to-server communications are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (HTTPS / SSL).
  • Server Directory Shielding: Sensitive backend directories (including logs, configuration files, and utility scripts) are strictly shielded from public web browsing via server access control rules (backend/.htaccess).
  • Abuse Prevention: Rate limiting and anti-spam honeypots safeguard form endpoints against automated attacks and denial-of-service abuse.
  • No Public Storage of Secrets: Application credentials, database keys, and configuration secrets remain server-side and are never exposed in client HTML, JavaScript, or public repositories.

Disclaimer: While we implement established industry standards to protect your personal information, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed 100% secure.

10.

Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Under applicable Indian laws—including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Information Technology Act, 2000—you have specific rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Inquire & Access: You may request confirmation of whether we hold personal data relating to you and obtain a summary of that information.
  • Right to Correction & Updating: You may request that inaccurate, incomplete, or out-of-date personal information be corrected or updated.
  • Right to Erasure: You may request the deletion of your personal enquiry data when it is no longer necessary for the purpose for which it was processed, subject to statutory record-retention requirements.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw your consent for future communication at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our practice by emailing aspirations@arkitect.in with the subject line “Privacy Rights Request”. We will acknowledge and review your request promptly.

11.

Children's Privacy

Our website and architectural engineering services are directed strictly to commercial entities, institutional bodies, developers, and adult individuals seeking architectural planning services. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18. If you believe a minor has submitted personal data through our website, please notify us at aspirations@arkitect.in so that we may promptly remove the information.
